Privacy Policy

This page explains how HARMONICS HUB collects, stores, and uses information submitted through the website.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to submit through our contact and academy registration forms, including your name, email address, phone number, service or course interest, and message details.

How we use submitted information

We use this information to review inquiries, respond to project or training requests, organize internal follow-up, and maintain a record of incoming business requests.

Storage and access

Form submissions are stored in our website database for business processing and follow-up. Access should be limited to authorized HARMONICS HUB team members who handle project, training, or support intake.

Your rights

You may request correction or deletion of information you have submitted by contacting hello@harmonicshub.com.
