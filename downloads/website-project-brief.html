Project Planning Worksheet

Website Project Brief

Use this worksheet to organize your website goals, audience, content priorities, and launch requirements before design or development begins.

Prepared by HARMONICS HUB Ideal for SMEs, schools, and service businesses Printable worksheet

How to Use This Brief

Answer these questions as clearly as possible before kickoff. A strong brief reduces rework, shortens approvals, and helps your website team design around real business priorities instead of assumptions.

What a Good Brief Covers

  • The main reason the website exists and the action you want visitors to take.
  • The audience segments you need to reassure, inform, or convert.
  • The core pages, proof points, and business information that must be present.
  • The assets, approvals, and timeline constraints that could affect delivery.

Fill-In Worksheet

1. Business name and primary contact
2. Main goal of the website
3. Target audience and what they need to understand quickly
4. Key services, products, or offers to highlight
5. Priority pages or sections
6. Trust signals available
7. Existing assets already prepared
8. Preferred timeline and ideal launch window
9. Success metric after launch
10. Notes, blockers, or approval considerations

Quick Prompt

If a visitor lands on your homepage today, what should they understand about your business in the first 10 seconds?

Before You Start

  • Gather your logo, brand colors, and approved business description.
  • List the pages your team already knows are essential.
  • Confirm who approves copy, design, and launch decisions.
A clearer brief usually means a faster website process and fewer costly revisions later.
