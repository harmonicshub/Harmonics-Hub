Training Needs Planner

Use this worksheet to define training outcomes, participant readiness, delivery format, and the practical support needed for a successful learning program.

Prepared by HARMONICS HUB Built for teams, schools, and learner cohorts Printable worksheet

How to Use This Planner

This planner helps you move beyond "we need training" into a clearer definition of who the learners are, what capability gap exists, and how success should be measured after the sessions end.

Questions to Clarify First

  1. What work problem, capability gap, or opportunity is driving this training request?
  2. Who exactly should attend, and what is their current starting level?
  3. What should participants be able to do differently after the program?
  4. What format, schedule, and support model will make follow-through realistic?

Fill-In Worksheet

1. Team, school, or learner group
2. Training objective
3. Skill areas required
4. Current level of participants
5. Preferred format and delivery environment
6. Preferred schedule or cohort pattern
7. Tools, platforms, or workflows involved
8. Expected business or learning outcome
9. Follow-up support needed after sessions
10. Internal notes, budget, or decision constraints

Outcome Check

Strong training plans define a visible skill gain, a practical project or workflow change, and a support path after the live sessions.

Helpful Inputs

  • List the tools participants already use today.
  • Note whether training should be beginner-friendly or role-specific.
  • Identify who will track completion and post-training adoption.
The clearer the training objective is, the easier it becomes to design useful sessions instead of generic presentations.
